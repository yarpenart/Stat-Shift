# Third-party artwork

Stat Shift contains modified versions of freely licensed vector artwork. The
source vectors are preserved in `assets/icons/*-base.svg`. The derived WebP
files add green/red backgrounds, resize the artwork, and recolor it for use as
Foundry VTT Active Effect icons.

## Peacock feather — CC0 1.0

`peacock-base.svg` is the *Peacock feather* vector from the Open Clip Art
Library, retrieved through Wikimedia Commons. It is dedicated to the public
domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peacock_feather.svg

The `peacock-success.webp` and `peacock-failure.webp` files are modified
derivatives of that public-domain vector.
